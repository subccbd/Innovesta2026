Payment & Verification Protocol

How to Pay & Confirm Registration

Please follow the designated payment method according to your registration category before October 17, 2026.

Method A

For Current Students (1,000 BDT)

1

Pay to Your Batch Representative

Pay 1,000 BDT in cash directly to your designated class/batch representative to confirm your registration before October 17, 2026.

2

Collect Payment Receipt / Serial

Receive your official physical receipt or unique verification serial number from your representative.

3

Submit Online Registration Form

Fill out the official Google form with your details and serial number before October 27, 2026.

4

Pass Confirmed

Offline verification is reconciled with your batch representative, issuing your All-Access credentials.

Registration Fee: 1,000 BDT
Method B

For Farewell Registrations (1,500 BDT)

1

Pay via bKash Send Money

Send 1,500 BDT via bKash Send Money to 01610181160 before October 17, 2026.

2

Save Transaction ID (TrxID)

Copy your bKash Transaction ID (TrxID) and take a screenshot of the successful transaction screen.

3

Submit Farewell Form with TrxID

Fill out the Alumni Farewell Google Form, entering your bKash TrxID and phone number before October 27, 2026.

4

Pass Confirmed

The registration desk verifies your bKash transaction and confirms your Grand Farewell Gala Pass.

Behind The Scenes

The Mystery Behind "Don't Scan This"

Before Innovesta was unveiled, cryptic neon teaser posters swept across the Southern University campus: "Don't Scan This — Unless You Dare to Know" and "You Have Been Exposed".

Every QR code led to the spark of an unprecedented movement. Innovesta 2026 is the answer — bringing together code, robotics, esports, alumni legacy, and musical celebration under one thrilling banner.
